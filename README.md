## Game loop
- Choose your fighter and opponent, enter the arena; move, attack, guard, dodge and special.
- Basic strikes randomly alternate among **horizontal slash**, **rising strike** and **forward thrust** (no identical back-to-back move), each with matched body interpolation and rotated/offset class-specific FX.
- **Land 3 consecutive ordinary hits** within a **3.8-second** chain window; the **4th regular attack input** triggers a short leap followed by a high-speed dash hit that damages and powerfully knocks back the target. Guarded/missed/parried hits do not count. The chain breaks if the attacker is hit or uses a special attack.
- AUTO takes over after **2 seconds** without manual input; it prioritizes ordinary strikes while building a four-hit combo.
- Opponent AI v3.1: roughly half the previous attack frequency, lower parry frequency, and a 0.92-second pressure/recovery window after each connected, unguarded player ordinary strike. The rival resumes normal fighting once the pressure expires.
- Victory/defeat key poses are held and shown on the results screen with dedicated previews.
- '다시 하기' rematches; after a win '계속 하기' advances; after a defeat 'Ludus로 돌아가기' returns to the title.

## Scope of the art
The 10 frames per fighter are **key poses**, not fully in-betweened 3D skeletal animation. The additional attack styles and leap-dash are runtime combinations of existing poses, body translation/rotation/scale and FX layers—not newly illustrated sprite sheets. Attack effects follow their own attacking fighter; the title roster order is Murmillo, Thraex, Retiarius. Current PNG art should be refined separately when clipped weapon edges, transparent fringe pixels, or art-layer misalignment is present. The source images themselves are not modified by a runtime layout update.

## Ludus hosted growth sprites (v4.5)

The 20 approved PNG atlases load automatically from `public/assets/ludus-growth/`: Thraex A/B, Murmillo A and Retiarius A, five stages each. PNG bytes are preserved exactly from the review ZIP. The adjacent manifest records actual packaged SHA-256 values and retains the supplied source hashes separately. ZIP import and IndexedDB image storage are no longer required. Failed image requests expose a retry button; arcade art remains the fallback. The Ludus hub preview follows the selected type and stage as images finish loading. Career saves, progression and arcade mode are preserved.

## Growth frame isolation (v4.6)

Ludus sheets are not uniform cells: long weapons and crests cross the nominal 4×3 boundaries, and some neighbouring poses touch. `frame-cuts.json` assigns source pixels to all 240 individual poses. It is generated by `scripts/build_growth_cuts.py` (Pillow, NumPy, SciPy) using full-sheet connected silhouettes and mask-constrained watershed for touching poses. Near-black exterior mattes in opaque sheets are excluded; original PNG files remain unchanged.

`public/growth-frames.js` copies only each pose's assigned original pixels into its own canvas with a 4px transparent gutter. Combat uses a common per-sheet scale, body-centred horizontal pivots and grounded feet. Ludus uses the isolated standing pose (frame 9). Arcade rendering, original atlases and career saves are unchanged. Metadata/image failures retain the existing arcade fallback and retry UI.

Validation: `node tests/growth-frames.cjs` (requires `@napi-rs/canvas`) checks all 240 canvases, PNG SHA-256 values, disjoint source ownership, transparent gutters and mirrored drawing. The generator writes contact sheets to `/tmp/gladiator-cut-review` for visual inspection. Artwork already clipped at the outer source-sheet edge cannot be reconstructed by cropping.

## Ludus roster and champion encounters (v4.7)

- The market stays open after the first hire. Select any owned fighter to train, equip or heal them independently; money is shared, XP/stats/equipment/injury are per fighter.
- Every match opens a deployment roster with the opponent's level and grade. Fighters with injury >=80 cannot deploy. The match snapshots its participant, so only that fighter receives XP, injury and record updates.
- Regular opponents randomly use the four growth variants; level 1–20 maps to stages at levels 1/5/10/15/20. The pending opponent is saved and retained after defeat or cancelling selection. Wins advance the challenge; losses do not skip bosses.
- Challenges 5/10/15 (and every fifth challenge thereafter) rotate the three original-art champions. The first win against each recruits that champion once. Recruits retain original art, use their combat idle pose in the Ludus, and can train/equip/deploy normally.
- Growth fighters use type/stage-specific face-scale corrections (0.64–0.94), identically for allies and opponents, with unchanged ground anchors and collision rules. Victory/defeat portraits now follow the fighter's actual growth appearance.
- Existing `gladiator.ludus.v1` saves are kept; `pendingOpponent` is additive. Arcade mode retains its existing art and opponent selection.

Run `node tests/ludus-roster.cjs` for roster, encounter, reward ownership, boss recruitment and arcade regression checks. It exercises the actual career/start functions with a small DOM test harness; it is not a browser playtest.
