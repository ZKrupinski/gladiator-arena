# METAL OF ROME v4 — Drums & the Crowd

## Approved direction

- Primary rhythm: "둥—둥—두둥", deep real war-drum, acoustic bass-drum and low-log-drum samples; 172 BPM underlying clock with half-time ceremonial heavy pulse.
- 24-bar, 33.488s synchronized loop: 4 original rhythmic variants, gradual intensity and fills.
- **No brass melody playing during combat**. Actual recorded brass remains limited to the ceremonial start/victory/defeat stingers approved in v3.
- Electric guitar reduced to sparse, optional low-register texture; no lead line, keyboards, flutes or pitched collision chimes.
- **Real human shouts**, not a horn tail or synthesized crowd. Live CC0 source by StarNinjas: https://opengameart.org/content/crowd-shoutingspeaking-ambience ; creator's source file `crowd_shouting_0.ogg` is resampled and remixed for an arena-sized stereo loop and two brief reactions.
- Base crowd in a **separate ambience bus**, ramped with distance between fighters, combos, low health and finisher; no constant loud cheering.
- On any blade swing, shield contact, hit, parry, dash or finisher **duck music and ambience** promptly to keep the combat actors audible.
- Low-pass and attenuate the looping crowd, so voice shouts remain background until a dramatic trigger.
- Preserves all previous sampled sword Foley and solemn entry/result horn cues.
- Source performer CC0 licensing: https://opengameart.org/content/crowd-shoutingspeaking-ambience
